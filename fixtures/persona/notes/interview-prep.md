# Interview prep (Northbeam, second round)

## Tell me about yourself
Frontend-leaning full-stack, six years, React and TypeScript, now at ElevenLabs on the customer dashboard.

## Story: slow CI
Blueport Labs, 2023. CI took 14 minutes per pull request and people batched changes to avoid waiting. My lead asked me to fix it without dropping tests. I split the Jest suite by package, cached node_modules and Docker layers in GitHub Actions, and ran Playwright in 4 shards. CI went down to 3 minutes and the number of merges per day doubled.

## Story: mentoring
Vistula Digital. I mentored Ola, a junior developer, through her first React project. Weekly pairing sessions, I reviewed every PR of hers and we kept a list of things to learn.

## Why Northbeam
I love that Northbeam's analytics is used by real logistics teams, and I want to work on data-heavy dashboards at your scale.
